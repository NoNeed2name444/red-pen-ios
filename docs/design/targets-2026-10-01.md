# Design targets from the owner, 1 October 2026

The owner sent four images on 1 October 2026 (iPhone, in the session chat). They are not in any
repository, so this file is the written record every later session builds against. The owner's words
are quoted; everything else describes what the images show.

## Decisions

- **No Rank card.** The Ward Round mock had a "Rank" card (Pre-clin, Clerk, FY1, Registrar,
  Consultant, "340 XP to FY1") and a "Clerk" badge on the Vitals screen. The owner: "remove them
  both" (1 Oct). No rank, level, XP or leaderboard anywhere; this matches the standing rule.
- **The app stays Stethoscore.** The mock used the name "Mnemonia"; removed by the same answer.
  Screens use `Brand.name`.
- **Where the 3D work goes.** The space, neurons and circuit images are labelled "Ideas map", so
  they target the app's 3D Ideas map. The performance theme is built there first as a
  self-contained renderer with no Stethoscore types in it, so the standalone 3D Knowledge Graph app
  (plan Task 10, new repo and brand) can take it later.

and the clinical metaphors (beds, charts, vitals, consults) where they fit. The three mock screens
are examples of the language, not its limits.

## 2. Space node styles: "this is how the shapes quality in the 3d app should be"

Photoreal, on a starfield; each style shown at rest and dragged upward. Links everywhere are glowing
orange-gold beams with fine filament particles.

- **Black hole (elevated):** black disc; photon ring Doppler-bright on the approaching side; lensed
  arcs; Keplerian streaks; the ISCO line. Beams bend in, redden and flash on arrival. Dragged: the
  disc flares and a bright plume of debris trails below.
- **Sun:** granulation, limb darkening, corona, prominences and a flare. Links run whiter and pulses
  leave as ejected plasma. Dragged: corona rays flare outward and plasma streams along the links.
- **Rocky planet:** day and night terminator from the light, city lights on the night side, a
  sunlight glint, a Fresnel atmosphere rim, a small moon. Pulses arrive as glints.
- **Gas giant:** banded clouds with jets, a storm spot, a ring casting the planet's shadow, and the
  ring's own shadow on the clouds. Links light a bar on the ring.
- **Pulsar:** a small tidy bright core, two sweeping blue-white beams, a purple-blue wind nebula.
  Its links ring in step with the beams.
- **Comet:** nucleus, a glowing cyan coma, ion and dust tails pointing away from the light. Icy dust
  sparkles along its links.

## 3. Ideas map, Neurons and Circuit themes

iPhone previews (393 x 852), high fidelity: dynamic lighting, depth of field, particles.

**Neurons.** The whole map: two brain regions (Cardiology, Examples), relays running down the
pathway; pegs as large glowing neurons, ideas as intermediate nodes; bioluminescent green, pink,
cyan and amber on deep blue with bokeh. Double-tapping Examples zooms into the region's pathway
(Examples, then Femoral and Inguinal), showing axons and synapses with active signalling.
Close-up, Heart failure to Acute coronary syndrome: two neurons, a burst of amber impulses along a
magnet-like axon from Heart failure, and a synapse flash on ACS as it arrives. Anatomy: a translucent
glowing soma with a purple and magenta interior, branching dendrites, the axon, synapse terminals.

**Circuit.** The whole board: a realistic green PCB with two sub-boards (Cardiology, Examples),
modules, capacitors, resistors, copper traces and gold buses between zones; a microchip labelled
like "CARDIOLOGY RP754-2G". Double-tapping Cardiology zooms into the region's pathway: pages as
capacitors and inductors, traces as links. Close-up: Acute coronary syndrome (a capacitor) sends a
burst of packets down its copper trace; the pad flashes and STEMI, an LED, lights as they land.
Other components: processors, ports, diodes, bus headers, blue electrolytic capacitors,
colour-banded resistors, LEDs. Real textures, reflections and materials.

**Re-sent the same evening as "the images of the quality upgrade".** The photoreal level of these
boards is the bar. Details the first reading left out:
- Screen controls in both themes: a back chevron at the top left, an "Ideas" title pill at the top
  centre, and three round glass buttons stacked at the bottom right (a list, a sparkle, a recentre
  target). Zoomed into a region, a pill such as "Examples · 14 notes" labels it.
- Neurons, "Other states (row 2): Gas, Pulsar, Comet... each with unique visual language and
  movement, showing different biological processes": the neuron theme has node variants matching
  the space styles, each with its own look and motion, not a recolour.

## 4. A performance theme: "focuses on performance as a priority to run at least 100000 nodes at once"

A theme for the Ideas map whose first priority is speed: at least 100,000 nodes with their links on
screen at once, at interactive frame rates on iPhone and iPad.

## 5. The app icon, in layers: "make each element of it separate to use in apple icon layout to run smooth iOS animations"

The icon stays the chosen render (design/icon/source.png): a glossy blue stethoscope with white
earpieces, silver metal and a chest piece marked "A+", on a sky-to-royal-blue rounded tile. Each
element becomes its own layer in an Icon Composer (.icon) file, so iOS 26 can light and move the
layers (Liquid Glass): the background, the tubing, the metal, the earpieces, the chest piece and
the A+.
