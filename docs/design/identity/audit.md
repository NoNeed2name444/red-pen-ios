# Identity audit: what is generic today, and where Stethoscore can stand apart

Written 15 Oct 2026. The screenshots cited are round 9 of the ward design, on the public branch `shots/ward-r9` at commit [`56787416`](https://github.com/NoNeed2name444/red-pen-ios/tree/56787416d5ca7c61d5d95d25547f52870b0d312f/shots), as `shots/<name>-light.png` and `-dark.png`. The competitor pages were read on 15 Oct 2026.

## 1. What in the app is generic today

Each item names the screenshot that shows it.

1. **Blue does the work of a brand.** Theatre Blue (`#1D5FB0`, `#2A6BC4` in dark) carries every action: "Start ward round", "Plan" and "+ New set" on [library](https://github.com/NoNeed2name444/red-pen-ios/blob/56787416d5ca7c61d5d95d25547f52870b0d312f/shots/library-light.png); the "Practice questions (MCQ)" title on [new](https://github.com/NoNeed2name444/red-pen-ios/blob/56787416d5ca7c61d5d95d25547f52870b0d312f/shots/new-light.png); the toggle, "Automatic" and the slider on [settings](https://github.com/NoNeed2name444/red-pen-ios/blob/56787416d5ca7c61d5d95d25547f52870b0d312f/shots/settings-light.png); the selected tab. Blue or teal is also the lead colour of AMBOSS, Osmosis, BMJ OnExamination, Anki, Medscape and Passmedicine (section 2), so the app looks like the category rather than like itself. In dark mode the accent becomes cyan, a second unrelated blue.
2. **A stock soft-UI ground.** The cool blue-grey `#E0E5EC` (dark `#2A2E35`) is the familiar ground of generic soft-UI kits. Under the ECG grid it reads as graph paper laid on a grey phone, not as paper ([library](https://github.com/NoNeed2name444/red-pen-ios/blob/56787416d5ca7c61d5d95d25547f52870b0d312f/shots/library-light.png), [library-se](https://github.com/NoNeed2name444/red-pen-ios/blob/56787416d5ca7c61d5d95d25547f52870b0d312f/shots/library-se-light.png)).
3. **No typographic voice.** The name is the system large title in bold, as in Settings or Notes; the only typographic gesture is the small-caps line above the rows ("TODAY'S WARD ROUND · 3 PATIENTS · 22 MIN"). Questions, scores and timers are set like any list ([library](https://github.com/NoNeed2name444/red-pen-ios/blob/56787416d5ca7c61d5d95d25547f52870b0d312f/shots/library-light.png), [quiz](https://github.com/NoNeed2name444/red-pen-ios/blob/56787416d5ca7c61d5d95d25547f52870b0d312f/shots/quiz-light.png)).
4. **A clip-art heartbeat.** The ECG beside the name is a small generic heartbeat glyph, the kind health and fitness apps use, drawn in `wardEcg` (`#BA273B`), a different red from everything else, and a different shape from the trace in the opening ([library](https://github.com/NoNeed2name444/red-pen-ios/blob/56787416d5ca7c61d5d95d25547f52870b0d312f/shots/library-light.png)). This is the line the owner asked to make detailed and the same colour as the opening.
5. **System icons in pastel tiles.** SF Symbols on tinted squares, with grey "Due" and "New" pills, are the default iOS list look ([library](https://github.com/NoNeed2name444/red-pen-ios/blob/56787416d5ca7c61d5d95d25547f52870b0d312f/shots/library-light.png), [new](https://github.com/NoNeed2name444/red-pen-ios/blob/56787416d5ca7c61d5d95d25547f52870b0d312f/shots/new-light.png)).
6. **Mode hues that read as system colours.** The seven section hues (blue, raspberry, green, teal, amber, violet, magenta) sit close to the iOS system colours at similar strength, so they read as defaults rather than as a family chosen for this app ([section-anki](https://github.com/NoNeed2name444/red-pen-ios/blob/56787416d5ca7c61d5d95d25547f52870b0d312f/shots/section-anki-light.png), [section-book](https://github.com/NoNeed2name444/red-pen-ios/blob/56787416d5ca7c61d5d95d25547f52870b0d312f/shots/section-book-light.png), [section-cases](https://github.com/NoNeed2name444/red-pen-ios/blob/56787416d5ca7c61d5d95d25547f52870b0d312f/shots/section-cases-light.png), [section-osce](https://github.com/NoNeed2name444/red-pen-ios/blob/56787416d5ca7c61d5d95d25547f52870b0d312f/shots/section-osce-light.png), [section-narrate](https://github.com/NoNeed2name444/red-pen-ios/blob/56787416d5ca7c61d5d95d25547f52870b0d312f/shots/section-narrate-light.png), [section-home-ideas](https://github.com/NoNeed2name444/red-pen-ios/blob/56787416d5ca7c61d5d95d25547f52870b0d312f/shots/section-home-ideas-light.png)).
7. **Ward role-play instead of exam words.** "Today's ward round", "Bed 1 · Cardiology", "Start ward round" ([library](https://github.com/NoNeed2name444/red-pen-ios/blob/56787416d5ca7c61d5d95d25547f52870b0d312f/shots/library-light.png)); "Patient 1 of 3", "Patient chart" ([quiz](https://github.com/NoNeed2name444/red-pen-ios/blob/56787416d5ca7c61d5d95d25547f52870b0d312f/shots/quiz-light.png)); "New admission" ([new](https://github.com/NoNeed2name444/red-pen-ios/blob/56787416d5ca7c61d5d95d25547f52870b0d312f/shots/new-light.png)). The student is preparing for an exam; the ward is the subject matter, not the setting. The full list is in [wording.md](wording.md).
8. **Every container looks the same.** Today's plan, each row, each option and each setting is the same white rounded rectangle with the same lift, so nothing says "start here" ([library](https://github.com/NoNeed2name444/red-pen-ios/blob/56787416d5ca7c61d5d95d25547f52870b0d312f/shots/library-light.png), [settings](https://github.com/NoNeed2name444/red-pen-ios/blob/56787416d5ca7c61d5d95d25547f52870b0d312f/shots/settings-light.png)).
9. **A quiz with no marking moment.** Options are plain capsules with a grey letter ("A Normal glomeruli"); being right or wrong is shown with the same green and red every quiz app uses ([quiz](https://github.com/NoNeed2name444/red-pen-ios/blob/56787416d5ca7c61d5d95d25547f52870b0d312f/shots/quiz-light.png)).
10. **A brand that appears once.** The stethoscope mark and its A+ appear in the opening and nowhere after it; home starts with a search field, like a utility.

What is already distinctive, and every route keeps: the stethoscope mark with the A+ on its chest piece; the ECG-paper grid; each mode's coloured frame and rims; the soft neumorphic rims on buttons; the lab value set in mono in a well ("Albumin 22 g/L L"); the "How sure? Sure · Maybe · Guess" row; and the exam words that are already right ("Best answer", "Timed", "Step 1 of 8", "Reveal").

What the table shows:

- **Colour.** Eight of the nine lead with blue, navy or teal-green. Only Geeky Medics avoids them, and it goes dark with a gradient. Nobody owns a warm paper ground, a red-ink hero, a signal orange or a plum.
- **Type.** All nine are humanist or geometric sans. A serif appears only in Medscape's editorial pages, and nobody uses mono for numbers.
- **Motif.** Brains (Geeky Medics' pixel brain, Quesmed's cartoon one), a triangle, a star, a play circle, bars. Nobody owns ECG paper, a calibrated trace, or the act of marking: the tick, the circled score, the margin note.
- **Voice.** Most promise a pass or mastery, two lean on magic or confidence, and Passmedicine sells comparison with other students. Quesmed alone speaks an examiner's language ("Mark Schemes").

## 3. Where Stethoscore can stand apart

1. **Colour: leave blue to the category.** A warm paper ground and a non-blue hero (red ink, signal orange or plum, one per route) is open ground. It also answers the owner's repeated flags on stray blue. Each mode keeps its own hue for its grid and rims, retuned as one family around the hero ([palettes.md](palettes.md)).
2. **Type: a voice for the exam.** Either an exam-paper serif for titles, scores and margin notes (Route A) or an instrument's expanded sans with mono numbers (Route B). Both are open in this category. Route C keeps Apple's rounded system face and earns its difference through material instead.
3. **Motif: ECG paper plus marking.** The grid is already the app's. The trace beside the name becomes the same complex as the opening, in one Trace colour, and marking (a tick, a circled score, a note in the margin) becomes how the app tells you how you did.
4. **Voice: an examiner on your side.** Plain exam words (paper, question, station, mark scheme, score, mock, revision, weak topics, pass mark, SBA, viva), calm and exact, never comparative. No ranks, leaderboards or peer histograms; the only comparison is with the pass mark and with yourself last week.
5. **The mark, used as a mark.** The stethoscope and its A+ stay exactly as drawn; the identity gives them a lockup with the name and the trace, so the brand lives on home, not only in the opening.
