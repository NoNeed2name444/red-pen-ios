# The owner's 10-colour palette (21 Oct 2026, task T0104)

Every accent and hue in the app is remapped onto ten colours; the neutrals
(grounds, cards, wells, text greys, grooves, the monitor, the faded row) are
unchanged. iPhone and Android take the same mapping.

## How it was chosen

- By role first, then nearest hue (OKLCH), so each of the seven sections
  keeps a colour of its own: Questions coral (23 degrees, was red 20), Cards
  blue (260, was indigo 274), Textbook teal (182, was 186), Cases peach (42,
  was rust 51), OSCE lime (129, was green 149), Audio lilac (294, was violet
  307), Ideas pink (350, was magenta 346).
- Cards takes Care Blue rather than Electric Indigo: Indigo sits 17 degrees
  from Soft Lilac, and the gate that no other section's colour reads as a
  blue (25 degrees from the primary's hue) would fail; Care Blue sits 34
  degrees from Lilac. Electric Indigo goes to the folder colours instead.
- Right is Fresh Mint (Pulse Teal is Textbook's), wrong Vital Coral,
  warnings Optimistic Amber, as asked. The sections' words stay at least
  0.076 in OKLab from each other and from those meanings (least gaps: 0.082
  by day, 0.081 by night); tools/ward_accents.py works the shades out and
  tests/ward/accent-blue.test.mjs and WardLookTests hold them to it.

## Text on a fill

White reads under 4.5:1 on every one of the ten (Electric Indigo 4.47, Care
Blue 3.68, the rest 1.5 to 2.8), so words on any fill are dark: the night
well's near-black #090E15 (`onPrimary`, 5.3:1 on Care Blue, the lowest of
the section fills). This is darker than the owner's "white on the others":
WCAG wins where white fails.

## Darker (or lighter) shades, for text only

The fills are the owner's hexes exactly, in both modes. Where a colour is
used as words or a glyph on a surface and does not read 4.5:1 on every tier
(3:1 for the beam and for folder glyphs), a shade of the same hue is used
for that text use only:

- By day every accent is a darker shade (the light tiers run #DDE2E8 to
  #F1F4F8, and none of the ten reaches 4.5:1 on them).
- At night the owner's colour itself is used where it reads 4.6:1 on every
  dark tier and on the section's own wash: Peach Glow, Lime Zest, Fresh Mint
  and Optimistic Amber. Vital Coral (4.46 on a sheet), Care Blue, Soft Lilac
  (4.55), Pulse Teal and Bubblegum Pink (under 4.5 on their own washes) take
  a lighter shade of their hue.
- Questions' words at night are a pale Vital Coral (#FEBAB5): the one coral
  that keeps clear both of Cases' Peach Glow and of a wrong answer's
  near-Vital Coral (#F57875).
- The red pen in exported Anki cards is #BB2A34 (5.8:1 on the card).
- Exported decks (DeckPalette, both platforms): a bar under white words is
  the lightest shade of the mode's fill that carries white at 4.5:1, and
  words in the mode's colour on the paper the lightest shade that reads 5:1
  on white (`ink`), where they were fixed 10 to 22 % shades of a darker tint.

## Not remapped

- The fill sheen in the owner's reference (a soft diagonal lighter wash):
  this task changes colour tokens only.
- The Space and Neurons scenes' rendered bodies (black hole, star tiers,
  planets' shaders, the microscope dyes and bokeh) and their legend swatches:
  they are physically modelled scenes, not accents; on Android likewise
  SpacePalette, the far points that grow into those bodies. The Space's
  node colours (the folder clusters) are remapped through NoteTone, and
  Android's graph nodes through MapPalette.
