# Android parity with the iOS app

What the Android app (Kotlin and Compose, `app/src/main/java/com/stethoscore/app/`) has of the iOS app it copies (`upstream/ios/RedPen/`), and the order the rest is ported in. The aim is a one-to-one replica, in shape and in function, of the latest iOS app.

Paths below drop the common prefixes: iOS files are under `upstream/ios/RedPen/`, Kotlin files under `app/src/main/java/com/stethoscore/app/`.

Verdicts: **same** (ported value for value, with tests), **differs** (exists on Android, but not as iOS has it), **missing** (nothing on Android yet).

## Where iOS can't be copied

- SwiftUI shadows and blurs: Compose has no inner shadow. Draw the inset lights with `drawBehind` and a blur-masked `Paint` (or `RenderEffect` on API 31+), from WardRelief's numbers.
- Dynamic `UIColor` per trait: Android uses a Compose `CompositionLocal` (`LocalWardLook`) and a Material scheme rebuilt per look; the glide between looks animates a `WardGlide` progress with `animateFloatAsState`.
- Asset catalogue colours (AccentColor, LaunchBackground): Android keeps them as `res/values` colours and the splash theme; the test checks the table against the iOS assets.
- The opening (LaunchScreen, LaunchPaper, LaunchSplash): Android's starting window and Android 12 splash stand on `ward_ground` (values and values-night, checked against WardGround by WardGroundTest); from the app's first frame the window's background is home's ground drawn as vectors (`ui/WardGroundViews.kt`, WardGroundDrawable), so there is no picture. The system's starting window cannot run app code, so it shows the ground's base without the grid; the app's mode is passed to the system (UiModeManager, Android 12 and up) so that colour is in the app's light or dark. There is no animated opening on Android yet.
- Swipe back: the edge swipe and predictive back are the system's back (on by default when targeting 36 and up), handled on every pushed screen by BackHandler (SwipeBackTest); nothing excludes the screen's edges.
- SF Symbols: Material Symbols (rounded), nearest glyph per symbol; SF Mono: `FontFamily.Monospace`.
- Sheets and the dock: `ModalBottomSheet` and a custom bottom bar, not `NavigationBar`.
- Speech (Narrate, Voice): `TextToSpeech` (Narrate reads with it, the word lit from `onRangeStart`) and `SpeechRecognizer` (it hears only the microphone, so it cannot transcribe a lecture file); a lecture's recording plays with `MediaPlayer` and is kept in the app's no-backup files; on-device Gemma: MediaPipe LLM Inference; Apple Foundation Models writer: the cloud writer only.
- Document scanner and camera (Lens, Library): ML Kit's document scanner and text reader are not in the offline build's libraries, so "Scan pages" is not offered (`DocumentScanning.isAvailable` is false, as in iOS's Playgrounds build) and Photos (the system photo picker) and Files bring the picture in; with no text reader, a picture arrives with no covers and takes iOS's "No labels were found" path. Lens's camera: CameraX, later.
- App Intents and widgets: app shortcuts and Glance widgets.
- Metal map (Notes): OpenGL ES 3.0 now, Vulkan later.
- StoreKit (Paywall): Play Billing, behind the owner's word.
- PDF contents links (DeckPDF): Android's PdfDocument has no link annotations, so the printed deck lists page numbers instead.
- Account sync: the backend transport (`network/BackendClient.kt`) is not automatic, conflict-preserving sync yet; that comes with Account (batch 19). APKG import and export (the library's Anki deck choice says so and asks for Notes in Plain Text), transcribing a recording (see Speech) and the accuracy UI are still missing too; cloud generation is the hosted client below (T0115a).

## The port, in day-sized batches

Look first, then home and library, each study mode, the rest. Each batch ports the iOS Foundation-only logic with its tests first, then the views, then screenshots against iOS's `shots/`.

1. **The Ward look's core** (done): WardPalette, WardSection, WardLook, WardRelief (but WardEdge) in `ui/ward/`, Theme.kt drawing every colour from them per section; WardPaletteTest and WardLookTest ported.
2. **Ward surfaces** (partial: the maths and its three suites done; the faces, the monitor's well and the paper frame drawn and in use; cached light images, slabs, the header band and bar strips left): WardEdge, WardIsland, WardLightSlice maths; raised, inset and rimmed faces, the paper frame, inset wells (WardSurfaces); WardReliefTests, WardIslandTests, WardLightSliceTests.
3. **Ward controls and type** (done): WardTokens' type, spacing and radii; WardControls (buttons, chips, pills, icon squares, segmented, fields, rings) with WardControlStates and the states gallery; the animated glide (WardLookDriver); WardStatesTests.
4. **Home** (partial: logic and its tests done, header, round and tiles drawn; band, mode tiles and exam card left): WardHome and WardPartLedger (paper, header, mode tiles, dock), the exam card and countdown; HomeFitTests (HomeFitTest) and WardRoundTests' goal and streak checks (StudyStreakTest) ported case for case. In `ui/home/` (WardHome, HomeFit, HomeRound), `ui/ward/WardPartLedger.kt`, and in `data/` StudyStreak (with GoalProgress and DailyGoal), BedPlan (with ExamWeekPlanner's phase), RhythmReading, WardWords. Differs: no WardPaperBand or masthead, and the name is set in text (no logo image); the mode and practise tiles are batch 5's; the countdown names the chosen exam (or the track) as iOS's does; the ExamDashboardCard (batch 16) stands on the Questions page as well as in the exam plan, where iOS has it; the planner mission, Plan button and exam-day kit bed are batch 14's (done); no account name in the greeting; study days come from answers and card ratings, not a study log; large text does not trim beds; the dock is unchanged and the cut is taken above it, as the list does not run under it; the ward-round clock checks are batch 14's (WardRoundTest).
5. **Library** (done, but what needs other batches): LibraryView, shelves, rows, search, tag chips, NewSetView and dock, import preview and incoming import, stats. Logic in `data/` (LibrarySearch with TextFold, CardTags and SourceSearch; CustomSession; QuizFromCards; PlainTextImport's tables; SetImport's received, rebased and combined; LibraryImport's preview and commit; LibraryStats: subjects, drill, calibration, reasons, the quizzes put together on the spot; StudyStore's folders, rename and combine); LibrarySearchTests and ImportTests ported check for check (LibrarySearchTest 5 cases, ImportTest 6). Screens in `ui/library/`: the list on home (sets, folders, Select, the row menu, the selection bar, search hits with scope chips, Build a session, Practise and Tools tiles), a mode's shelf, By subject (StatsScreen and its question lists), New set in three steps with its dock (typing and Open a file: Quizlet / CSV tables through the import preview, a Stethoscore .json set), tag chips, and tables or backups opened from another app (MainActivity's intent filters, IncomingImportHost). Not yet: the lecture path and writers, card editors and Turn into (batch 6), .apkg decks (an Anki deck asks for Anki's Notes in Plain Text instead), the readiness card and rule sheet (batch 15), reasoning tools (batch 17), picture-from-photo (batch 6), How it works (Support). Home's own empty-library card stays (batch 4), so the library's "Make your first set" is not drawn there; the Cards page's Due cards tile is home's, so Practise on Cards is left empty here.
6. **Library editors** (done, against iOS fed0105; the photo picker stands in for the document scanner): CardsEditorView and CardEditSheets (`ui/editors/CardsEditorScreen.kt`: Edit cards or questions, swipe, hold or Edit's minus to delete, Save changes forgetting a deleted card's reviews; the card and question sheets, a cloze with nothing hidden and a question with no answer marked cannot be saved), TagChips (`TagChips.kt`), OcclusionCoverEditor (`OcclusionCoverEditor.kt`: move, stretch by a corner, draw), PictureFromPhotoView (`PictureFromPhotoScreen.kt`, from the Cards page's tile), MCQGenerateForm, LecturePDFSection and LectureWriterSection (`MCQGenerateForm.kt`, `LecturePDFSection.kt`, `LectureWriterSection.kt`, for New set's steps 2 and 3). The logic in `data/PhotoOcclusion.kt`, `data/CardTags.kt`, `data/MCQEdit.kt` and `data/LibraryEditing.kt` (CardStyle, cloze holes, MCQCoverage, ReadSource, the lecture forms' words, Word and PowerPoint text). PhotoOcclusionTests, DataIOTests' tags, SlideTests' editing and CoverageTests' suggested count ported (PhotoOcclusionTest, CardTagsTest, MCQEditTest, LibraryEditorsTest), with LibraryEditorsScreenTest. Not yet, for want of the feature on Android: a writer other than Stethoscore Cloud (T0115a; with none chosen the forms take iOS's no-model path and say so), labelled-diagram finding and its picture cards, reading a scanned page's words, a PDF's words before Android 15, the floating dock action, Gemma download, the paywall; New set (batch 5) is to host the three lecture forms.
7. **Questions** (done; what is still missing is listed in the `Features/MCQ/` row): MCQQuizView with faded wrong rows, MCQExamTools, MCQSummaryView, ChartQuiz.
8. **Cards** (done): AnkiReviewView, AnkiCardFace, DueTodayView, ReviewCardActions, FSRS, against iOS dcb4745. Scheduling in `data/AnkiScheduler.kt` (ReviewPlan: queue, study ahead, Due across decks, merge, prune) and `data/ReviewOptions.kt` (FSRS-5, limits, suspend, bury, undo, Flagged holds, a bury that keeps 4 am across a clock change); the screens' words and sitting rules in `data/CardReview.kt`; `ui/AnkiReviewScreen.kt` (AnkiReviewScreen, DueTodayScreen, and the Cards page's Due cards tile), `ui/AnkiCardFace.kt` (face, Reveal, rating bar), `ui/ReviewCardActions.kt` (Undo chip, hold or swipe for Bury and Suspend). ScheduleTests and ReviewEssentialsTests' review checks ported (ScheduleTest, ReviewOptionsTest), with CardReviewTest and AnkiReviewScreenTest. Plain Compose in the Ward colours until batches 2 and 3 bring the shared surfaces and controls (the header, study bar, big buttons, empty state and finish are private stand-ins). Not yet, for want of the feature on Android: Quiz me, Check accuracy and its badge, Save to Ideas, opening a citation's page, Due today's Listen, the .apkg schedule rows.
9. **Textbook and Sources** (done): BookReaderView, figures, highlights; SourcesLibraryView, SourceDocumentView, SourcePageReader, reader bar, credits, against iOS fed0105. Logic in `reading/`: BookPages (split, blocks, tidyPage), BookFigures (assign, compact, reference, rebased), Highlight, SourceSearch and Citation (accent-folding search, citation to page), SourceKinds and Provenance, ContentCredits (the six content sources with iOS's credit lines, SetCredits, the exam style banks). Screens in `ui/book/BookReaderScreen.kt` (opens set kind Book) and `ui/sources/` built on the Ward port: SourcesLibraryScreen (Settings, Your lectures), SourcePreviewScreen with SourceDocumentView and ReaderBar, SourcesCreditsScreen (ContentLicencesScreen in Settings, About; SetCreditsScreen on route `credits`). SourceTests, ReadingTests and ContentCreditsTests ported case for case (SourceTest, ReadingTest, ContentCreditsTest), with ReaderWordsTest and ReaderScreensTest for the screens. Differences: no lecture files on Android, so the reader is text pages only (no PDF, Word or PowerPoint pages, Quick Look or Save to Ideas); the textbook has no More menu, accuracy badge or Draw from memory (Guess first is batch 14's, offered on a book's first opening); the credits read the exam's name and style bank from ExamCatalog (batch 16); the library's menu entry for a set's credits is batch 5's; a card's citation does not yet open its page (batch 8). FigureTests (figure grid, occlusion from slides) belong to batch 6's editors.
10. **Cases** (done): CaseListView, CaseWardView, CaseDecisionView, CaseDebriefView, CaseMaker and run store. The typed CaseFile, CaseNames (with iOS's seeded candidate shuffle, bit for bit), CaseChecks and CaseChart, CaseRun and CaseScore, CaseDebrief, CaseWriting, CaseSamples and the case parts of AccuracyRules and AccuracyItem in `data/` and `accuracy/`; CasesTests ported check for check (CasesTest) with the shuffle's parity added; the four screens in `ui/cases/`. Differences: the case writer is Stethoscore Cloud only (T0115a: CaseMaker.writer(backend), with none "Another patient like this" says so); no AccuracyHolds store, so only a case marked held is held; the chips, segmented control, option rows and faces are plain Compose in Ward colours until the Ward controls are used here.
11. **OSCE and Mock** (done): OsceReviewView, OsceGenerateSection; MockPaperView, MockSittingView, MockResultsView, MockSittingSave. The station drill (`ui/osce/OsceReviewScreen.kt`: hidden step, Reveal, Start over or I got it, the exam's station clock with its two taps, the finish naming the steps started over at, resume from `StudyStore.osceProgress`) and the generate section (`ui/osce/OsceGenerateSection.kt`: PDF, Word and PowerPoint read for their words, the count, More options, the stations appended to the text) on `data/OsceStations.kt`; the mock (`ui/mock/`: the paper and its resume card and past sittings, the sitting with its strict section clock, flags, grid, crossing out, highlighter and exam tools, kept on disk after every change, the results with subjects weakest first, every answer, and Practise missed) on `quiz/MockPaper.kt`. OsceTests, MockSittingSaveTests and ExamToolsTests' mock cases ported (OsceTest, MockSittingSaveTest, ExamToolsTest). Differences: no AI writer (Make says so), no target exam or blueprint, no spoken patient, accuracy badge, Save to Ideas or review prompt; a PDF's words need Android 15.
12. **Audio** (done, against iOS fed0105): NarrateReviewView, listening and reading, the spoken word's wash, audio bar, fix-word sheet, lecture importer. The logic in `audio/`: AudioRate, SleepChoice and SleepCountdown, AudioChapters (AudioControls), NarratePlan, FlowLines, WordTiming, LectureTranscriber and LectureLanguage, SoundKey, PronunciationStore and OnDeviceLearning, TranscriptCorrections, LectureAudio. The screen in `ui/narrate/` (NarrateReviewScreen, replacing ClinicalScreens' NarrateScreen): Line X of Y, the words flowed one target each (tap to jump, hold to fix), the line being read pressed in and the word being said washed, the sections and sleep row, the recording's scrub bar, back 15, Play and forward 30 with its speed, or the reading controls with Add audio, the fix sheet and its report with Undo, More's Add or Replace recording, the engine choice and the importer. AudioControlsTests, NarrateTests, TranscriberTests, LearningTests and LectureAudioTests ported case for case, with CloudTranscriptTests' cases that are not Gemini's (TranscriberTest) and NarrateScreenTest. Differences: the phone's voice only, and Pause restarts from the word; Gemini and the on-device transcriber both say they are not set up on this phone (no paid AI, no recogniser for a file), and the kept recording is loaded at once rather than on the next opening; no lock-screen controls, Guess first, Ward pocket or sync-safe save; speeds are kept in the app's preferences and pronunciations in `redpen-pronunciations.tsv` in its files.
13. **Ideas** (partial, against iOS fed0105; Neurons, the Universe's folder plan and sparks left): IdeasView (`ui/ideas/IdeasView.kt`: search, folders with Rename, New folder inside and Delete, swipe to delete or move, the List / Board / Space switcher folding into a disc, the capture bar with New idea, New page and New folder), IdeaBoardView (`ui/ideas/IdeaBoard.kt`: pan, pinch, cards washed in the folder's colour, curved or straight links in lanes, hold or Connect to join and part, drag to move, double-tap to add, Back to the middle), NoteEditorView (`ui/ideas/NoteEditor.kt`: kind and folder, Write or Read as Markdown, `[[` suggestions, linked notes and backlinks, Link to…, Turn into cards, Delete; full screen, not a sheet), and on the GL map the Space look menu (theme, Universe or one style for all, folder looks, Lines, the legend), Performance with the 100,000, 300,000 and 1,000,000 demos, and the depth fade with its level of detail (SpaceDepth, off when the focus is at or behind the eye, the iOS defect fixed). Logic in `ideas/` (IdeaBook for NoteStore, IdeaLines, IdeaLooks, NoteMarkdown, NoteEditing); LineStyleTest, IdeaBookTest and SpaceDepthTest ported. Still to come: the Neurons theme (GraphNeurons and its suites), styles from the Universe's folders (a note's links stand in), the peek card, sparks (SpaceOptics.trail is ported, not drawn: Android's map has no moving bodies) and curved links on the map.
14. **Learn** (done, against iOS fed0105): WardRoundView and overlay, GuessFirstView, BedtimeReviewView (with MorningCheckView), ExamPlanView, ExamDayKitView, SymptomBlocksView, LearnRoutes. Logic in `data/`: WardRound (the clock as dates, StudyMinutes), RetentionForecast, SecuredRule, StudyRhythm (with ReminderSettings and LearnDefaults), SymptomBlocks, Pretest, and ExamWeekPlanner's situation, mission, papers and checkpoints in BedPlan. Screens in `ui/learn/`: a learning sheet over the app (LearnRouter) with Done; the exam plan from home's Plan (phase, today's streak and goal ring, the ward round tile, forecast, due bars, locked-in rings, phase actions); the exam-day kit from the kit bed or the plan; guess first over a textbook or narrate set opened for the first time (a wrong answer faded grey); bedtime re-read drawn dark; morning check; symptom blocks with their mix-ups. The ward round's clock (WardRoundClock) keeps its round in SharedPreferences, counts what is studied while it runs (StudyStore.onStudied), adds its minutes to the streak and floats its chip over every screen, dragged to any corner, opening into its panel. Home's round takes the planner's mission, untried count and the track's pacing. WardRoundTests' clock checks, LearnTests and BedPlanTests ported case for case (WardRoundTest, LearnTest, BedPlanTest; LearnTest's exam cap reads the store's exam date, Android having no ExamTrack date key). Differs: no notifications for a round's end or the daily rhythm (ReminderSettings is ported without its Settings screen), no Live Activity or Health minutes; the plan's Your exam card, Rule sheet and Ward pocket open batch 16's and 15's screens; bedtime's line is the explanation's first sentence (not the rule sheet's rule); the kit paces the exam track's paper, not the chosen exam's; a deck gets no pretest from its cards; the chip's throw is aimed from the drag's velocity, not UIKit's predicted end.
15. **Vitals and Insight** (done, against iOS fed0105): AnalyticsView, AnalyticsVisuals, VitalsParts, WardVitals; InsightViews, InsightExamples, RuleSheetView; Coverage. The Vitals screen (`ui/VitalsScreen.kt`, its numbers in `ui/vitals/VitalsNumbers.kt`) on the Ward port: the vitals line, the rhythm monitor (WardMonitorCard, RhythmTrace in green), the four WardRings, the consult card, Focus next with its sparklines, the readiness card and range bar, the weekly and daily charts, the reasons donut and calibration chart, the wrong answers with their subject picker, the subjects with trend arrows, and the time figures and 12-week calendar. The readiness estimate (`data/Insight.kt`: Readiness, ported sum for sum) also fills home's Vitals ring. The rule sheet is kept in the library file with iOS's keys. BedPlanTests ported case for case (BedPlanTest) and AccessibilityTextTests' calendar steps (InsightTest), with Android's own checks of the rest (InsightTest, VitalsWordsTest). Not here: By subject (batch 5), the AI writer and the cloud syllabus check (no sign-in), a chosen exam's weighted blueprint on the syllabus page, and Differentiate Without Colour's dots (Android has no such setting).

The catalogue of 21 exams with their blueprints, formats and marking (`exam/ExamCatalog.kt`), the choice kept beside the track (`exam/ExamChoice.kt`), the blueprint as coverage map, readiness, study next and quotas (`exam/ExamBlueprint.kt` over `coverage/CoverageEngine.kt` and `coverage/Syllabus.kt`), the exam's papers, pacing and blueprint-weighted mock (`exam/ExamFormats.kt`), the Ward pocket's calculators and scores (`exam/WardPocket.kt`, `exam/WardPocketScores.kt`); screens in `ui/exam/`. ExamCatalogTests and WardPocketTests ported check for check (ExamCatalogTest, WardPocketTest). Not ported, for want of the feature on Android: ExamCatalogTests' exemplars (no bundled exemplar index), prompts (no MCQGenerator) and accuracy cut-offs (no AccuracyModel); the onboarding question (no FirstRunView); a question writer behind Study next and Practise this.
