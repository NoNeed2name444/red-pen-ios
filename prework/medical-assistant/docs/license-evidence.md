# B1 source-license evidence

Access date for all pages below: 2026-10-07. This is bounded catalog and policy evidence gathered for the named sources. It is not legal advice or an item-level license attestation. The source-level catalog entries are kept separate from manifest eligibility in `data/manifests/license-report.json`; `verified_license` remains null and each source remains `unknown` until an actual ingested item has matching item ID, scope, and direct evidence as required by `scripts/prework.py`.

## Screening outcome

No dataset files were fetched or imported. No source or item is marked approved. All five source entries have empty item-level `evidence`, null `verified_license`, and `status: unknown`. The CBIS-DDSM catalog record is the only exact named dataset collection among the supplied source names, and its CC BY 3.0 evidence is recorded at collection scope only. The immediate blocker for all five is the absence of concrete item IDs and item-specific rights statements. For IDC and NIH, a dataset/repository collection identifier is also needed before a relevant catalog policy can be selected. For LibreTexts and Springer OA, the exact page/article plus separately credited included material must be checked for each proposed item.

## Contract checked

`scripts/prework.py::license_flags` allows only `unrestricted` or CC-BY 1.0/2.0/2.5/3.0/4.0, and requires `verified: true`, `scope: item`, a matching `item_id`, and nonempty evidence entries with matching item ID, URL, and terms. No source catalog page recorded here meets that item contract by itself. Therefore the report intentionally retains exclusion flags rather than relaxing the pre-ETL gate.
