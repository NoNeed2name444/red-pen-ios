## The three items left open

1. **Theatre Blue in comments.** All are comments, and there are more than
   the three files reported: 46 lines in 29 non-test Swift files. One
   example is WardSurfaces.swift:553, where `wardControls()` calls the tint
   "the Theatre Blue" when it is now the section's accent. The only code
   literal is Brand.swift:49, `Brand.mark` = #1D5FB0, and nothing outside
   Brand.swift uses it. No colour on screen comes from any of these.
   **Not wrong, only stale.**
2. **make_icon.py.** Its WORDMARK (tools/make_icon.py:42-43) keeps the old
   inks and draws "score" in #1D5FB0 by day and #7DB0F2 by night. The
   committed LaunchLogo PNGs carry exactly those colours. I decoded them:
   the most common saturated pixels are 1D5FB0 and 7DB0F2. The ground is
   transparent (corner alpha 0), and the relief is drawn as alpha layers,
   so there is no box on the new LaunchBackground. But the first frame still
   shows a Theatre Blue "score" before the app opens in indigo, so the
   commit's "Theatre Blue leaves the app" holds everywhere but the launch
   screen. **Inconsistent, not broken; it needs a follow-up outside this
   allow-list.**
3. **LibraryView.swift:485 `?? .questions`.** It cannot be reached.
   StudyCategory (StudyCategory.swift:11) has questions, cards, cases, osce
   and audio, and each is a WardSection raw value, so
   `WardSection(rawValue:)` never fails. **Nothing bypasses the palette.**
