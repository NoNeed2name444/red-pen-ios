## Risk R1 (plausible, not blocking, not a repair)

The nit 3 change keeps every row that slides below the cut while moving,
not only the holder. Each such row stays in the book at its last moving
position, which is within one scroll step below the cut.

Scratch simulation:
1. The holder is at 680. Row X is last seen at 683 while moving and is then
   dropped. After the rebook the tops are [680, 683], and the cut stays at
   680, so this is harmless.
2. The rows then shift up at rest (the holder is booked at 660). X's stale
   entry now holds the cut at 683 instead of 686. The next rebook keeps X,
   because the cut it holds also hides it.

The old code dropped X while moving, so this hold-up is new. It is the
same harm nit 2 describes, now reachable after any scroll back that is
followed by a shift at rest. The effect is under 12 pt per step, it
cannot cause a loop, and it does not occur in the preview gate. Narrowing
the change, for example keeping a moving drop only if its rest entry was
already hidden, would bring back the flash after a long scroll. So I
record the risk for the next review and do not ask for a change.

Nit 2's pruning has a matching limit. It removes kept parts once the cut
sits above them. It cannot tell a ghost that holds the cut from a real
hidden holder.
