## Ruling on the push-and-back fault (item 4)

**The check's rule is wrong. Home's parts are not lingering because of a leak.**

1. **The pushed page has no cut of its own here.** `WardPartBook.shared.cut` has
   one writer, home's own page: `LibraryView.swift:526`
   (`.onChange(of: pageCut, initial: true) { _, cut in WardPartBook.shared.cut = cut }`).
   The pushed set page neither sets the cut nor books parts. `wardPartsProbed`
   is set only on home's List (`LibraryView.swift:800`), and the book held 7
   parts before the push and 7 at its last look. So 543 is home's own cut,
   worked out again while home was covered. On ax5-library-se-light it rose
   127 pt above its resting 670. On the other layouts that kept parts, the kept cut
   equals the resting one (AX1 682.33, AX5 phone 640). The report doesn't say why it rose;
   that is worth a later look, but it doesn't decide this check.
2. **Under either cut, every drop is kept.** Every face booked on that layout
   (682, 971, 1142, 1219, 1765, 1812, 2071, from `home-fit-small.txt` in
   shots/ward-nits-3) lies at or below both 670 and 543. So `WardPartLedger.drop`
   (`WardPartLedger.swift:122-126`) keeps each part that goes away, whichever cut
   it is handed. That is the ledger's design (third review, N4): a part that
   disappears wholly below the cut is hidden, not gone, so the cut cannot fall
   back and loop. A kept part cannot move the cut, because `HomeFit.settledCut`
   (`HomeFit.swift:73-84`) only rises to tops above it. After the pop all 7 parts
   were back: "fits", no "parts lost", and the page ends at 670 as at rest.
3. **The three kept drops already prove the push.** The fourth review asked the
   gate to prove that the push really covered home. Three of home's views went
   away (`onDisappear` → `WardPartBook.drop`) between `resetDropCounts()` and the
   pop. On a layout where every part sits below the cut, a removal can never
   happen, so "some part left the book" is the wrong question to ask there.

**What the check should require instead.** Home counts as covered when either:

- at least one of home's parts left the book during the push (a removal, or a
  booked key gone, exactly as now); or
- every face booked before the push lay wholly at or below home's cut at that
  moment (the ledger's own test, `top + 1e-6 >= cut`), and at least one part went
  away and was kept.

Three cases stay as they are:

- If nothing went away at all, the check still FAULTs.
- Where any part stood above the cut, a kept drop never stands in for a
  removal. That is exactly where a cut that moved while home was covered could
  hide a part that should have left, so the old strength stays there.
- The per-name "parts lost after a push and back" check is unchanged.

All nine reports in shots/ward-nits-3 pass under this rule, and a trip that
never covers home still fails.

### N-R1, the exact change (design/ward-nits)

   ```swift
       /// Whether a push and back showed home covered by the pushed page: a
       /// part of home left the book during the push, or, when every face
       /// booked before it lay wholly at or below home's cut, at least one
       /// went away and was kept by that cut, all the book can show then
       /// (WardPartLedger.drop). `faces`: the tops of the faces booked before
   ```

## design/ward-island: REPAIR

### I-R1: the fold icon at large text (required)

On a joined deck the chevron is a fixed 17 pt (`Theme.swift:593`), which is
`.body`'s size at the default text size. At accessibility sizes the text grows
to 28-53 pt while this icon doesn't, and nothing like the Large Content Viewer
makes up for it. So yes, it is an accessibility regression at large text. It
also makes the same control behave one way on a joined deck and another way
elsewhere.

The old `.body` wasn't right either: at AX sizes it overflowed its fixed
44×28 pill, which `foldClearance` measures (`WardIsland.swift:76-100`). The
icon should grow with the text up to the largest size the pill holds, on every
deck, with the Large Content Viewer beyond that. That is Apple's pattern for a
fixed-size bar control.

- `ios/RedPen/Shared/Theme.swift:591-593`. Old:
  ```swift
                  // The joined deck's icon stays within its measured pill at
                  // accessibility sizes; the band's hit target is still 44 pt.
                  .font(inset > 0 ? .system(size: 17, weight: .semibold) : .body.weight(.semibold))
  ```
  New:
  ```swift
                  // Grows with the text up to the largest size its measured
                  // 44 by 28 pill holds, on every deck; past that the Large
                  // Content Viewer shows it. The band's hit target is 44 pt.
                  .font(.body.weight(.semibold))
                  .dynamicTypeSize(...DynamicTypeSize.xxxLarge)
  ```
- `ios/RedPen/Shared/Theme.swift:602`. Old: `        .contentShape(Rectangle())`
  New:
  ```swift
          .contentShape(Rectangle())
          .accessibilityShowsLargeContentViewer()
  ```

### The island, checked (item 3)

- **Geometry is solved when it should be, not per frame: holds.**
  - `WardEdge.join` runs only in `WardEdgeStore.resolve`. That is reached from
    `update` when a host's frame or `shown` changes, and from `setPage` and
    `setWindow` when the page, the sky or the window changes
    (`WardSurfaces.swift:262-325`). It reuses the last answer when the panel,
    the sides and the host's minY, minX and maxX are unchanged (line 319).
  - Canvases and Animatable views only interpolate the 13 solved numbers
    (`WardEdgeVector`).
  - `foldInset`'s 40-step bisection runs in `WardDeckSlab`'s body (line 919).
    That body runs when the view updates, not on each drawn frame, because the
    modifier isn't Animatable.
  - One cost to note: both canvases rebuild `hole.strokedPath` on each drawn
    frame of an animation (lines 478, 832). That draws paths but solves
    nothing.
- **No pixel inked twice: holds, with one exception and one assumption.**
  - In the 4-pt band, the frame's line and the clearance pieces share one
    `compositingGroup` with one opacity (line 540), so a shared pixel is never
    darker than the line.
  - At the page's edge, the two sides are cut by complementary non-AA clips
    (lines 476, 479).
  - *Assumption:* this only works if the navigation container really clips the
    page at x = 16. `WardIsland.swift:30` assumes it, and `plan.md:1547` was
    unsure ("if the navigation stack clips there"). `WardPageEdge` spans
    `-sideInset ... width + sideInset`, so if the page is not clipped, the band
    is inked twice.
  - *Exception:* where a low deck's arc meets a bottom corner inside the page,
    `WardPageEdge` cuts out the frame's stroke with a non-AA clip (line 833).
    The frame's own anti-aliased edge pixels are still inked by both layers,
    for about 10 pt along the tangent, and the stair-stepped cut shows through
    the 0.55 line. This is minor.
- **Sheets, covers and the sky: hold.**
  - With the sky covering the window, `sides` is false
    (`WardIsland.swift:13`), so there is no entry, and the line and strip fall
    back to today's `Rectangle` and base fill.
  - A sheet or cover joins only if its bar's sides are within 1 pt of the root
    page's (`WardIsland.swift:16-17`). The tests cover this ("sheet and sky
    have no pieces"). The test is geometric, though: a cover whose bar sat
    exactly 16 pt in would join a frame it hides.
- **Animations, judged from the code (nothing was run here).**
  - Scroll edge appearing: no animation (`WardScrollTopEdge`). The line, arcs
    and pockets appear at once, as the old line did. Fine.
  - Grow and checked: the store's change runs in the host's captured
    transaction (line 761 → `update`), so the pieces glide with the deck.
  - Fold: removed at once, and so is the page's line. Fine.
  - Rotation: re-solved and snapped along with the layout. Fine.
  - iPad Split View: re-solved at each step of a resize, with 18 pt corners on
    iPad (`WardPaper.padRadius`; tests "A1 iPad ... including narrow window").
  - Reduce Motion: no animation anywhere, so everything moves together. Fine.
  - Unfold: a new entry has its animation stripped (line 297). The frame's band
    pieces appear at once at their final y while the deck's line is still
    rising, so they stand apart for the length of the unfold.
  - Keyboard: depends on whether `.transaction { clock.value = $0 }` sees the
    keyboard's transaction. That can't be judged from the code.
  - Push and pop (including an interactive back swipe): the band pieces live in
    the window's overlay and follow SwiftUI's model frames, while UIKit moves
    the pages. Nothing ties the pieces to the page's line during the move.
- **The deck slab, clipped and masked.**
  - Controls: none is cut. The fold pill and its 5-pt rim are kept 2.1 pt clear
    of the slab's curve and the island (`foldInset`, tested at every size). The
    deck's buttons sit 12 pt in from the slab's sides (`Theme.swift:550-553`),
    and their rims (5 pt) stay inside even the 31-pt top corners.
  - The relief *is* cut. The open deck is lit: only the folded button sets
    `wardUnlit` (`Theme.swift:534`). Its `wardRaised(lift: .high)` paints its
    highlight and shade outside the shape. On every joined deck,
    `face.clipShape(shape)` (`WardSurfaces.swift:925`) removes all of that, and

    anyway. So a joined deck's slab no longer stands out from its strip, which
    goes against the owner's "keep ... the soft relief". It is not in the plan
    note.
  - This needs the owner's call. Either record the flat joined slab as their
    decision, or keep the relief inside the island: draw the relief under a
    mask that runs past the slab by the relief's reach, cut only by the
    island's outline. There is no exact one-line repair, so it is not counted

- **Tests.** The new `wardisland` suite and the `wardrelief` additions check
